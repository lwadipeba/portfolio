AI · NLP · Product Development

Brief Byte

An AI-powered briefing tool designed to help people stay informed without becoming overwhelmed by the volume of daily news and newsletters.

TeamSeven members
ToolsPython, Streamlit, Hugging Face
Output5–10 minute briefings

The challenge

Useful information is often buried beneath volume.

Brief Byte brings multiple newsletter and RSS sources into one experience, identifies relevant content and produces a concise briefing based on the reader’s interests and available time.

The product was designed with traceability in mind. Users retain access to the original source links and the system includes an extractive fallback when a generative summary is unavailable.

How it works

From many sources to one useful briefing

1. Collect

Ingest articles from selected newsletters and RSS sources.

2. Rank

Use TF-IDF and cosine similarity to match content with reader interests.

3. Summarize

Use DistilBART with an extractive fallback to create a time-controlled briefing.

5–10minute briefing options

1streamlined reading experience

Determinants of Happiness

A study of the economic, social and institutional factors associated with national happiness using data from 137 countries in the 2023 World Happiness Report.

Dataset137 countries
MethodOLS regression
Expanded model84.6% R²
GitHub ↗Access slidesRead paper

Research question

What explains why some countries are happier than others?

The project began by testing GDP as a single predictor, then expanded the model to account for social support, healthy life expectancy, freedom, generosity, corruption perceptions and regional differences.

The results suggest that financial resources matter, but the quality of people’s social and institutional environments provides substantial additional explanatory power.

Methodology

Analysis process

Prepare

Cleaned the 2023 dataset, reviewed missing values and standardized variable interpretation.

Model

Estimated a GDP-only baseline and an expanded multivariable model with regional controls.

Evaluate

Compared explanatory power, coefficient direction and model assumptions.

The findings identify associations rather than causal effects. Country-level averages may also conceal important differences within countries.

Waymo vs Uber

After repeatedly using and loving Waymo, I wanted to look beyond the novelty and investigate whether autonomous ride-hailing could be a genuinely viable alternative to Uber.

ComparisonPrice and wait time
Safety records814 reports reviewed
Routes10 Los Angeles trips
GitHub ↗Read actual paper ↗

The question

Does a better ride experience translate into a viable service?

I compared Waymo and Uber across price, wait time, route performance and safety. The analysis shows a meaningful tradeoff: Waymo was less expensive on average, while Uber offered much shorter and more consistent wait times.

The project also examined incident reports carefully, separating the growth in total reports from injury severity and geographic concentration.

Pangaea

A unified AI workspace for USC MSBA students that transforms fragmented academic, career and personal signals into one clear next step.

ProductAI student operating system
PilotSix weeks
Core outcomeOn-time completion
Open prototype ↗Explore slides

The student story

Tommy has everything he needs, but it lives in too many places.

A reading response sits in the LMS. A problem set deadline is buried in a syllabus. A recruiter email needs a response. A case competition registration is closing. Pangaea brings these signals together, removes duplicates and recommends the most useful next action.

Every signal. One clear next step.

Directional research

Students wanted help prioritizing, not another place to check.

68%unsure what to do next several times a week
72%dissatisfied with current organization systems
80%want prioritized academic deadlines
80%+check numerous platforms

Small directional student cohort; results guide the pilot rather than represent the full student population.

Important Mail

Surfaces messages that require action.

Academics

Prioritizes deadlines and workload.

Tracks recruiting and networking actions.

Personal

Protects personal commitments and availability.

AI workflow

From fragmented information to one recommendation

Extract

Read approved email, LMS, calendar and document sources.

Understand

Clean, connect and remove duplicate signals.

Prioritize

Consider deadlines, workload, availability and career relevance.

Act

Recommend one next step with supporting context.

The six-week analysis uses a baseline week, four live weeks and a final readout. The proposed evaluation uses intention-to-treat and difference-in-differences.

AI Product Case Competition · The Fourth Dimension

DealCheck

An AI brand-deal manager that helps independent influencers understand what they will earn, what they must deliver and what they might risk before accepting a partnership.

TeamLame Wadipeba, Helen Li, Leo Liu, Joshya Gupta
AudienceIndependent creators
OutputBrand Deal Health Report
Open prototype ↗

The opportunity

The headline fee hides the real cost.

Independent creators make business decisions using information scattered across contracts, email, calendars, financial records and social analytics. Production expenses, unlimited revisions, exclusivity, broad usage rights and payment delays can materially change a deal’s true value.

DealCheck connects this information and helps creators decide whether to accept, negotiate or decline an offer.

The business intelligence of a manager before creators can afford one.

Evidence

Creators face financial, capacity and audience pressure.

67%earned under $10,000 annually from content creation
48%reported receiving late payments
78%said burnout affects motivation
42%reported audience-brand tension

Sources represent separate CreatorIQ, Lumanu and Patreon/NewtonX populations and are not pooled.

Product framework

Three connected assessments

Financial Fit

Extracts compensation, expenses, payment timing and rights, then estimates effective hourly compensation.

Schedule Fit

Compares campaign obligations with available production capacity, deadlines and revision buffers.

Brand & Audience Fit

Checks alignment with the creator’s values, audience, competitor restrictions and creative freedom.

Brand Deal Health Report

Every report includes contract evidence, missing information, confidence indicators, prioritized negotiation points and an editable response.

Product rule: Missing critical information prevents a Green recommendation. A strong fee cannot outweigh an impossible deadline or prohibited brand category. Financial urgency remains separate from deal quality.

Proposed pilot

Test speed without sacrificing understanding.

≥95%critical-field extraction accuracy
